Symphony of the Eclipse

Main campaign

Guided by a mysterious sigil to a portal beneath a crimson moon, the Phoenix Vanguard entered the realm of Eclipse in search of answers, only to find themselves drawn into a brutal conflict against the Crimson King and his servants as they fought to uncover the truth behind the sigil that seemed inexorably linked to their fate.
